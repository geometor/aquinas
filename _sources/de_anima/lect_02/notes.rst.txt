Commentary & Notes — On Sound in Act and Potency and Its Fluent Mode of Being in Motion
=======================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Aristotelian Sources, Euclidean Counterparts, Hoenen's Noetics, and GEOMETOR Significance*

Aristotelian & Scholastic Foundations
-------------------------------------

Commenting on :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`\ 's *De Anima* II.8 (419b4–420a19), Aquinas situates acoustics within Aristotle's hierarchy of the external senses: sight perceives through a purely spiritual or intentional immutation of the pellucid medium, whereas hearing perceives through local motion propagated across air or water. Aquinas's methodological prologue (no. 438) coordinates the entire Aristotelian *libri naturales*—cross-referencing *De Sensu et Sensato* for color, flavor, and odor, *De Generatione et Corruptione* for tangible contraries, and *Meteorologica*—to explain why sound alone must be analyzed *in fieri* within *De Anima* itself: because sound possesses no static accident residing in the bronze bell, its formal actuality is inseparable from the kinetic propagation of percussion through an undivided medium.

Euclidean & Classical Geometric Counterparts
--------------------------------------------

Aquinas's exposition of acoustic propagation and reverberation (nos. 446–449) deploys explicitly Euclidean geometric models of radial symmetry and catoptric reflection. First, the diffusion of compressed air from the locus of percussion is modeled as a family of concentric circular waves (*gyrationes*) expanding outward from a center (:person:`Euclid <euclid>`, *Elements* I, Def. 15), where wave intensity varies inversely with radial distance. Second, Aquinas pairs acoustic echoes from smooth or concave vessels with the optical reflection of light rays from water and polished bronze (*aes*, rendering :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`\ 's χαλκός at 419b31), invoking the geometric law of reflection systematized in the Euclidean *Catoptrics*—where incident and reflected rays make equal angles with a smooth surface, while concave surfaces converge and multiply reflected impulses and rough opaque surfaces scatter light diffusely so as to prevent total shadow outside direct solar rays.

Hoenen's De Noetica Geometriae & Judgment Theory
------------------------------------------------

In the metaphysical Appendix to *De Noetica Geometriae* ('Additions,' nos. 1–2, citing *In II De Anima*, lect. 16 alongside *In IV Physicorum*, lect. 17 and *De Potentia*, q. 7, a. 2, ad 9), :person:`Hoenen <hoenen>` elevates Aquinas's contrast between permanent qualities and sound lacking 'a fixed and resting *esse* in the subject' (no. 438) into a general theory of existential modes of being. Motion strictly speaking possesses a continuous *fluent being* (*esse fluens*) whose successive duration and variable velocity exhibit quantitative and structural proper attributes analogous—yet irreducibly heterogeneous—to permanent spatial extension. Tracing a line from Aquinas and Nicole Oresme's medieval 'configurations of qualities across temporal duration' to modern wave mechanics and quantum photons, Hoenen shows how periodic, oscillatory variations in *fluent being* explain acoustic pitch and optical color without collapsing four-dimensional spacetime calculations into a univocal geometric :term:`continuum <continuum>`.

GEOMETOR Constructive & Symbolic Significance
---------------------------------------------

Aquinas's contrast between permanent, resting form (*esse permanens et fixum*) and *fluent being* (*esse fluens*) propagating in concentric circular waves directly mirrors the interplay between static structural invariants and dynamic wave-like propagation in GEOMETOR constructions. In ``geometor.model``, each compass construction radiates from a struck center across a chosen radial distance, generating new intersection loci step by step along a directed acyclic graph whose expanding rings echo the concentric *gyrationes* of Lecture 16. While the completed symbolic model retains permanent algebraic being—every golden ratio proportion and harmonic cross-ratio discovered by ``geometor.divine`` holds timelessly—the constructive sequence animated in ``geometor.explorer`` unfolds in *fluent being*, allowing the inquirer to witness how local percussions of point and radius reverberate across the entire geometric field.
