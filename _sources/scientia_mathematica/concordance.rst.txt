Sister Repository Bridges Across GEOMETOR
-----------------------------------------

geometor-hoenen — P. H. J. Hoenen, S.J., De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis (Romae: Apud Aedes Universitatis Gregorianae, 1954)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Cites St. Thomas Aquinas across 78 distinct locus keys to establish that geometric axioms and theorems arise through intuitive formal abstraction (*abstractio intuitiva formalis*) within spatial phantasms (*materia intelligibilis*) prior to propositional judgment.

geometor-pacioli — Fra Luca Pacioli, O.F.M., De Divina Proportione (Venice: Paganino Paganini, 1509), Pars I, Capp. II–VII
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Echoes the Aristotelian-Thomistic hierarchy of speculative sciences (*Super Boetium De Trinitate* qq. 5–6 and *Posterior Analytics* I.1): mathematical science stands in the *primo grado di certezza* ('first degree of certainty'), abstracting continuous and discrete quantity to contemplate the Divine Proportion ($\phi$) and the five regular Platonic solids.

geometor-euclid / geometor-elements — Euclid, Elements (Books I–XIII; Byrne 1847 color diagrams & symbolic DAGs)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Supplies every concrete geometric paradigm analyzed by Aquinas: Definitions of point, line, surface, circle (*Metaph.* VII.10–11); Postulates & Common Notions (*Anal. Post.* I.5, I.18–19); equilateral construction I.1 (*Anal. Post.* I.2); congruence I.4 (*Phys.* IV.7); triangle angle-sum I.32 & semicircle right angle III.31 (*Metaph.* IX.10); and diagonal incommensurability (*Metaph.* V.9).

geometor-model / geometor-explorer / geometor-divine — GEOMETOR Symbolic Engine, Interactive Phantasm Canvas, and Golden Ratio Discovery
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Computationally embodies Thomistic geometric noetics: `geometor.model` encodes pure *materia intelligibilis* in exact SymPy algebraic fields; `geometor.explorer` renders the determinate visual *phantasma* required by *conversio ad phantasmata*; and `geometor.divine` performs *inventio per divisionem in actu* to uncover emergent golden sections.
