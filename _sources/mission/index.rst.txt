mission
=======

``geometor.aquinas`` recovers, translates, and annotates the foundational writings of **St. Thomas Aquinas, O.P. (1225–1274)** on mathematical science, *materia intelligibilis* (intelligible matter), abstraction, constructive division of figures in act, and judgment terminating at the imagination.

Our mission is threefold:

1. **Primary Philological Fidelity**: Present 100% unabridged, verbatim public-domain Latin texts from the critical Leonine, Marietti (Pirotta, Cathala–Spiazzi), and Decker editions alongside rigorous, paragraph-synchronized English translations.
2. **Complete Hoenen–Euclid Concordance**: Ground all 78 Thomistic citations in Fr. Peter Hoenen S.J.'s *De Noetica Geometriae* (``geometor.hoenen``) and Fra Luca Pacioli's *De Divina Proportione* (``geometor.pacioli``) directly in their unabridged Aristotelian-Thomistic contexts and Euclidean theorems (``geometor.euclid``, ``geometor.elements``).
3. **Epistemology of Symbolic Constructive Geometry**: Articulate why exact symbolic computation in ``geometor.model``, interactive diagram inspection in ``geometor.explorer``, and golden-ratio invariant discovery in ``geometor.divine`` realize in modern computational form Aquinas's timeless synthesis of *intellectus*, *phantasma*, and *divisio in actu*.
