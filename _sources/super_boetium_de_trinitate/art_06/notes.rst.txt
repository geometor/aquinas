Commentary & Notes — Whether in Divine Things Imagination Must Be Altogether Relinquished (Why Mathematical Judgment Terminates at the Imagination)
===================================================================================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Aristotelian Sources, Euclidean Counterparts, Hoenen's Noetics, and GEOMETOR Significance*

Aristotelian & Scholastic Foundations
-------------------------------------

Question VI, Article 2 brings Aquinas's theory of scientific judgment to completion by uniting *On the Soul* III.7–8 (431a16–b2, 432a8–14: the soul never understands without a *phantasm*, and :term:`phantasms <phantasma>` stand to the intellective soul as sensible objects to sense), *On the Heavens* III.7 (306a5–17, where :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` rebukes the Platonists for forcing physical elements into geometric polyhedra in defiance of sensory observation, insisting that physical science must terminate at sensory appearance), *On the Soul* I.1 (403a12–16, alongside *Metaphysics* III.2, 997b35–998a4, refuting Protagoras's cavil that a straight ruler touches a bronze sphere across a sensible expanse rather than at a point), and Pseudo-Dionysius's *On the Divine Names* and *Mystical Theology* (the triplex via of causality, eminence, and negation).

Euclidean & Classical Geometric Counterparts
--------------------------------------------

Aquinas's concrete example in *co. 3*—that a mathematical straight line touches a sphere at a single indivisible point alone, whereas a physical ruler touches a material sphere along an extended patch—corresponds directly to :person:`Euclid <euclid>`\ 's *Elements* III.16 (a straight line drawn at right angles to the diameter of a circle from its extremity falls outside the circle and touches it at one point alone) extended stereometrically to the sphere (as in Theodosius's *Sphaerica* I.3). By distinguishing what external sense perceives in rough bronze or chalk from what constructive *imagination* exhibits under the governance of a *definitive notion* (*ratio diffinitiva*), Aquinas explains how Euclidean diagrams function in proof: the geometer never measures chalk thickness with empirical calipers (*ad sensum*), but judges exact point-incidence and tangency by terminating judgment at idealized spatial imagination (*ad imaginationem*).

Hoenen's De Noetica Geometriae & Judgment Theory
------------------------------------------------

Chapters III and IV of :person:`Hoenen <hoenen>`\ 's *De Noetica Geometriae* (1954) constitute an extended philosophical commentary on the exact doctrine of *q. 6, a. 2, co. 3* and *ad 6*. Examining :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`\ 's and Aquinas's response to Protagoras on tangent lines and angle sums (*In III Metaph.* lect. 7, no. 416; *In I Anal. Post.* lect. 42, no. 7; *ST* I, q. 84, a. 6–7), Hoenen demonstrates how *terminari ad imaginationem* avoids both empiricist skepticism (which denies exact points and lines because sensation is coarse) and Platonic or Hilbertian formalism (which severs geometry from spatial intuition altogether). Because the *phantasm* remains a *permanent foundation* (*permanens fundamentum*, *ad 6*) illuminated by the :term:`agent intellect <intellectus-agens>`, the geometer inspects within imaginative extension itself the exact indivisibility of limits and necessary incidence relations, verifying universal geometric judgments in constructive intuition.

GEOMETOR Constructive & Symbolic Significance
---------------------------------------------

Aquinas's twin theses in Question VI, Article 2—that the *phantasm* abides as a *permanent foundation* (*permanens fundamentum*) of intellectual insight, and that mathematical judgment *terminates at the imagination* (*terminari ad imaginationem*) rather than at coarse empirical sensation—define the exact synergy between *geometor.model* and *geometor.explorer*. Whereas floating-point CAD or dynamic geometry software reduces tangency and intersection to numerical pixel tolerances (*ad sensum*, where a tangent line and circle may blur or miss due to rounding error), *geometor.model* adjudicates every incidence according to its exact *definitive notion* in SymPy symbolic algebra—verifying, just as Aquinas writes in *co. 3*, that a tangent line meets a circle at a single exact algebraic point—while *geometor.explorer* renders luminous SVG diagrams and animations as precision visual *phantasms* in which the human intellect directly inspects and contemplates the intelligible structure of the construction.
