Commentary & Notes — Whether Speculative Science Is Fittingly Divided into Natural, Mathematical, and Divine
============================================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Aristotelian Sources, Euclidean Counterparts, Hoenen's Noetics, and GEOMETOR Significance*

Aristotelian & Scholastic Foundations
-------------------------------------

Aquinas constructs his division of theoretical philosophy by synthesizing :person:`Boethius <boethius>`\ 's *De Trinitate* (c. 2) with three canonical Aristotelian loci: *Metaphysics* VI.1 (1026a10–19), which establishes physics, mathematics, and theology according to separability and immobility; *Physics* II.2 (193b22–194b15), which contrasts the physicist's inclusion of :term:`sensible matter <materia-sensibilis>` with the mathematician's consideration of surfaces, volumes, lines, and points apart from motion; and *Posterior Analytics* I.2 and I.7–13, which demand immobile necessity of scientific objects and govern the subalternation of *quia* sciences to *propter quid* sciences. Crucially, Aquinas enriches this Greek framework with :person:`Avicenna <avicenna>`\ 's *Liber de philosophia prima* (I.1–3) and *Canon medicinae* (I.1.1), refining the distinction between the ontological :term:`subject-genus <genus-subiectum>` (*ens commune*) and the formal *ratio considerandi*, while integrating :person:`Ptolemy <ptolemy>`\ 's *Almagest* (I.1) and Hugh of Saint Victor's *Didascalicon* (III.3) to situate logic and the quadrivium cleanly as methodological propaedeutics.

Euclidean & Classical Geometric Counterparts
--------------------------------------------

In replying to the third objection (*ad 3*), Aquinas characterizes geometry and arithmetic within the quadrivium as liberal arts because they exercise an immediate *opus rationis*—numbering and measuring—within pure reason itself. This directly illuminates the dual propositional structure of :person:`Euclid <euclid>`\ 's *Elements*, traditionally divided since Proclus into *theorems* (demonstrating eternal properties of figures) and *problems* (constructing figures from postulates, such as bisecting an angle or constructing an equilateral triangle in *Elements* I.1 and a square in *Elements* I.46). Unlike mechanical arts that manipulate recalcitrant physical timber or bronze, Euclidean straightedge-and-compass postulates enact an interior rational construction whose intelligibility depends neither on :term:`sensible matter <materia-sensibilis>` nor on physical force, while subalternating applied optical, harmonic, and astronomical treatises (*Optics*, *Sectio Canonis*, *Phaenomena*) by supplying their *propter quid* quantitative demonstrations.

Hoenen's De Noetica Geometriae & Judgment Theory
------------------------------------------------

In Chapter VII of *De Noetica Geometriae* (1954), Peter :person:`Hoenen <hoenen>` S.J. singles out *Super Boetium De Trinitate* q. 5, a. 1, ad 3—paired with *In I Anal. Post.* lect. 2, no. 5 and lect. 15—to resolve modern axiomatic controversies (notably Bertrand :person:`Russell <russell>`\ 's critique of *Elements* I.1) over mathematical existence. Hoenen observes that while continuous magnitude (*magnitudo*) is the fundamental presupposed :term:`subject-genus <genus-subiectum>` of geometry, determinate figures such as the equilateral triangle and the square function as secondary subjects whose non-contradictory mathematical existence cannot merely be assumed nominally, but must be proved through *quasi-operative* rational construction (*opus ipsius rationis*). For Hoenen, Aquinas's recognition of an interior constructive work within speculative mathematics explains how Euclidean postulates bridge pure theoretical contemplation and generative geometric synthesis without lapsing into pragmatism or conventionalism.

GEOMETOR Constructive & Symbolic Significance
---------------------------------------------

Aquinas's isolation of mathematics as a speculative science possessing an immediate interior *opus rationis*—measuring, numbering, and constructing apart from :term:`sensible matter <materia-sensibilis>` while remaining distinct from arbitrary human fabrication—captures the exact philosophical identity of the GEOMETOR ecosystem. In *geometor.model*, compass-and-straightedge operations are enacted as pure rational acts that do not approximate physical ink or pixels, but unfold necessary algebraic consequences latent within exact symbolic space. Simultaneously, Aquinas's account of subalternation in *ad 5*—wherein arithmetic and geometry supply the *propter quid* reasons for harmonic and astronomical phenomena—anticipates the architecture linking *geometor.model* to *geometor.divine* and *geometor.phyllotaxis*, where pure radical proportions such as the golden ratio provide the formal explanatory ground for harmonic ranges and natural botanical symmetries.
