Commentary & Notes — Whether One Must Proceed Rationally in Natural Things, Disciplinably in Mathematics, and Intellectually in Divine Things
=============================================================================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Aristotelian Sources, Euclidean Counterparts, Hoenen's Noetics, and GEOMETOR Significance*

Aristotelian & Scholastic Foundations
-------------------------------------

Expounding :person:`Boethius <boethius>`\ 's famous methodological triad (*De Trinitate* c. 2: *in naturalibus rationabiliter, in mathematicis disciplinaliter, in divinis intellectualiter*), Aquinas synthesizes *Posterior Analytics* I.27 (87a31–37, arithmetic as more certain and prior to geometry, which exists *ex additione* by adding position to unit), *Physics* I.1 (184a16–21, proceeding from sensible effects better known to us toward causes better known by nature), *Metaphysics* II.3 (995a12–16, learning the mode of science before science itself, and demanding mathematical rigor only of immaterial subjects), :person:`Ptolemy <ptolemy>`\ 's *Almagest* (I.1), Boethius's *Consolation of Philosophy* (IV, pr. 6; V, pr. 4), and Pseudo-Dionysius's *On the Divine Names* (Ch. 7). He integrates these with the Neoplatonic-Scholastic method of *via resolutionis* (analysis back to simplest extrinsic or intrinsic principles) and *via compositionis vel inventionis* (synthesis descending from first principles to conclusions).

Euclidean & Classical Geometric Counterparts
--------------------------------------------

Aquinas's analysis of mathematical demonstration in *co. 3* and *co. 11–12* directly reflects classical Greek geometric practice from :person:`Euclid <euclid>` to Pappus. In *co. 3*, Aquinas notes that mathematics demonstrates solely through the formal cause (intrinsic definition), and remarkably anticipates an objection from Euclidean auxiliary constructions: when a geometer proves a property of a circle through an inscribed or circumscribed triangle or vice versa (as in *Elements* IV and XII.2), he does not invoke an extrinsic physical cause, because *in the circle the triangle exists potentially, and conversely*—echoing :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`\ 's *Metaphysics* IX.9 (1051a21–33) on geometrical discovery actualizing latent divisions! Furthermore, Aquinas's coupling of *via resolutionis* and *via compositionis* in *co. 22* mirrors the classical geometric pair of *analysis* and *synthesis* codified in Pappus's *Collection* Book VII (*Treasury of Analysis*).

Hoenen's De Noetica Geometriae & Judgment Theory
------------------------------------------------

:person:`Hoenen <hoenen>` explicitly cites *Super Boetium De Trinitate* q. 6, a. 1 in Chapter V of *De Noetica Geometriae* (1954, line 132: *both sciences—metaphysics and logic—are common, and in a certain way concerning the same subject*) while distinguishing *logica docens* from *logica utens* (*co. 1–2*): in building geometry, logic in use becomes the living form immanent within concrete mathematical demonstrations, governed by objective laws of being rather than arbitrary syntactic conventions. Moreover, in Chapters I, IV, and VII, Hoenen builds upon Aquinas's vindication of mathematics as *disciplinalis* par excellence (*co. 11–12* and *ad 14*, where mathematical reason *receives from the imagination*): geometry serves :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` and Aquinas as the *archetypus scientiae* precisely because its formal-cause demonstrations actualize latent nexus in imaginable extension with immediate, indubitable clarity.

GEOMETOR Constructive & Symbolic Significance
---------------------------------------------

Aquinas's striking remark in *q. 6, a. 1, co. 3*—that when geometry demonstrates properties of a circle from a triangle or conversely, it does so because *the triangle exists potentially in the circle and conversely*—resonates profoundly with constructive discovery in *geometor.model*, *geometor.explorer*, and *geometor.divine*. Every step of a compass-and-straightedge construction in *geometor.model* actualizes points, segments, and polygons already latent *in potentia* within the algebraic field of the initial configuration; *geometor.divine* then performs a systematic *resolutio*, scanning the completed construction to uncover emergent golden sections and harmonic cross-ratios that flow by pure formal necessity from the underlying definitions, exemplifying :person:`Ptolemy <ptolemy>`\ 's and Aquinas's *demonstration wrought by indubitable paths*.
