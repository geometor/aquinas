Commentary & Notes — On the Origin of Science from Sensible Things, Turning to Phantasms, and Threefold Abstraction
===================================================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Aristotelian Sources, Euclidean Counterparts, Hoenen's Noetics, and GEOMETOR Significance*

Aristotelian & Scholastic Foundations
-------------------------------------

Written at the height of Aquinas's maturity (c. 1267–1268, contemporary with his *Sentencia libri De anima* in Part III of this Codex), Questions 84 and 85 orchestrate a grand historical dialectic among :person:`Democritus <democritus>` (*De Somno*, *De Generatione* I.8), :person:`Plato <plato>`, :person:`Augustine <augustine>` (*83 Different Questions*, *De Genesi ad litteram* XII), :person:`Avicenna <avicenna>`, and :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` (*De Anima* III.4–8, *Metaphysics* Z.10–11, *Posterior Analytics* II.19). By grounding human cognition in the substantial unity of the rational soul as *forma corporis*, Aquinas explains why our intellect occupies the exact ontological midpoint between bodily sense (bound to individual corporeal matter) and angelic intelligence (proportioned to separate forms): its proper object is the :term:`quiddity <quidditas>` existing in corporeal matter, which must be abstracted from individuating conditions by the *intellectus agens* yet contemplated in concrete particulars via *conversio ad phantasmata*.

Euclidean & Classical Geometric Counterparts
--------------------------------------------

Question 85, Article 1, ad 2 codifies the epistemological charter of Euclidean geometry within the hierarchy of sciences: *numeri et dimensiones et figurae, quae sunt terminationes quantitatum*—precisely the subject-genera of :person:`Euclid <euclid>`\ 's arithmetic (Books VII–IX), plane geometry (Books I–VI), and stereometry (Books XI–XIII)—can be considered apart from all sensible qualities (hot/cold, hard/soft) because dimensive quantity inheres in corporeal substance prior to sensible qualities, yet cannot be understood without substance subject to quantity (*materia intelligibilis communis*). Simultaneously, Question 84, Article 7's phenomenological appeal—that whenever we strive to understand or teach someone else, we form exemplary :term:`phantasms <phantasma>` (*exempla*) in which to inspect the universal truth—describes the universal pedagogical structure of Euclid's *Elements*, where every general *protasis* ('In any triangle...') is immediately instantiated in a singular lettered *ekthesis* ('Let *ABC* be a triangle...').

Hoenen's De Noetica Geometriae & Judgment Theory
------------------------------------------------

:person:`Hoenen <hoenen>` builds Chapters II and III of *De Noetica Geometriae* directly upon *ST* I, q. 84, a. 6 (and ad 3, concordance key *aquinas:st:I.q84.a6*), q. 84, a. 7, and q. 85, a. 1, ad 2 (concordance key *aquinas:st:I.85.1.ad2*), just as :person:`Lonergan <lonergan>` centers *Verbum* Chapter 1 ('Insight into :term:`Phantasm <phantasma>`\ ') upon *ST* I, q. 84, a. 7! Hoenen emphasizes two revolutionary insights in these texts. First, because sensible cognition is only the *materia causae* while the *intellectus agens* supplies higher active power (*q. 84, a. 6, co. & ad 3*), intellectual judgment draws from defective sensible diagrams exact geometric limits—such as indivisible points and breadthless tangent lines (answering Protagoras's objection that sensible circles touch a ruler across a segment rather than at a single point)—that transcend raw sensory data while remaining objectively true of extended continua. Second, *q. 85, a. 1, ad 1–2* proves that geometric abstraction is an intuitive inspection of formal structure (*simplex et absoluta consideratio*) within *materia intelligibilis*, never a mendacious Platonic hypostatization.

GEOMETOR Constructive & Symbolic Significance
---------------------------------------------

Aquinas's twin doctrines of *abstractio a materia sensibili* (*q. 85, a. 1, ad 2*) and *conversio ad phantasmata* (*q. 84, a. 7*) map directly onto the dual architecture pairing *geometor.model* with *geometor.explorer* (and, in abstract visual reasoning, *geometor.seer* with *geometor.seer_navigator*). *geometor.model* executes pure mathematical abstraction by stripping away every floating-point pixel artifact or physical imperfection, representing points, lines, and circles in exact algebraic species over *materia intelligibilis*; yet to comprehend, teach, and heuristically explore those symbolic structures, human reason requires *geometor.explorer*'s visual SVG diagrams—concrete exemplary :term:`phantasms <phantasma>` (*per modum exemplorum*) in which the intellect directly beholds universal proportional harmonies instantiated in a particular construction.
