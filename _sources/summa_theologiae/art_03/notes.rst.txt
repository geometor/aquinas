Commentary & Notes — That Esse Is the Actuality of All Acts and the Perfection of All Perfections
=================================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Aristotelian Sources, Euclidean Counterparts, Hoenen's Noetics, and GEOMETOR Significance*

Aristotelian & Scholastic Foundations
-------------------------------------

*De Potentia* q. 7, a. 2 (disputed at Rome c. 1265–1266) represents Aquinas's most daring metaphysical synthesis, weaving together :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` (*Metaphysics* IV, V.7, *Posterior Analytics* I–II, *On Interpretation* I.1), :person:`Boethius <boethius>` (*De Hebdomadibus*), Hilary of Poitiers, Moses Maimonides (*Guide of the Perplexed* I.57), Pseudo-Dionysius (*De Divinis Nominibus* V), and the Neoplatonic *Liber de Causis* (Propositions 4, 8, and 9). Confronting Objection 9's Aristotelian-sounding claim that *esse* is the most indeterminate and imperfect substrate—analogous to prime matter awaiting categorical determination—Aquinas effects a profound metaphysical revolution: far from being minimal potency, *esse* is the ultimate act (*actualitas omnium actuum*) without which every substantial or accidental form remains merely potential in matter, in an agent's power, or in an intellect's concept, while finite essences contract *esse* as potency limits act.

Euclidean & Classical Geometric Counterparts
--------------------------------------------

In Reply 11 (*ct_id* 60122), Aquinas anchors his crucial epistemological distinction between propositions self-evident in themselves (*per se notae secundum se*) and propositions self-evident to us (*quoad nos*) in :person:`Euclid <euclid>`\ 's Fifth Common Notion (*Elements* Book I, Common Notion 5, cited via *Posterior Analytics* I.2): *Omne totum est maius sua parte* ('Every whole is greater than its part'). Because the predicate *greater than its part* is analytically contained in the intelligible *ratio* of a quantitative whole, anyone who grasps the geometric meaning of *totum* and *pars* immediately assents to the axiom once its terms are known (*cognitis terminis*); by contrast, although in God *esse* is identical with His essence, our intellect lacks direct quidditative vision of God's essence and must therefore demonstrate the propositional truth 'God is' (*an est*, Reply 1) from created effects.

Hoenen's De Noetica Geometriae & Judgment Theory
------------------------------------------------

*De Potentia* q. 7, a. 2, ad 9 (concordance key *aquinas:de-potentia:7.2.ad9*) is the centerpiece of :person:`Hoenen <hoenen>`\ 's entire existential commentary in the Appendix to *De Noetica Geometriae* (cited four times across lines 303–362!). Hoenen subjects Aquinas's phrase *hoc quod dico esse* ('this that I call *esse*') to meticulous logico-noetic analysis: placed in the grammatical subject as a substantive infinitive (*Peri Hermeneias* I, lect. 5), *esse* signifies an authentic intellectual conception belonging to simple apprehension, drawn via resolution from concrete experience; yet when affirmed in the second operation of the mind, *esse* is posited as the existential act corresponding proportionally to each quidditative nature or relation—from permanent subsistent essences down to continuous spatial magnitudes and even the fluent, oscillatory *esse* of sound and light!

GEOMETOR Constructive & Symbolic Significance
---------------------------------------------

Aquinas's distinction in *De Potentia* q. 7, a. 2 between a form considered merely in potency or in conceptual definition versus a form posited in actual *esse* (ad 9)—paired with his distinction between *an est* as propositional verification and *quid est* as essential structure (ad 1 and ad 11)—illuminates the verification philosophy of GEOMETOR. Defining an abstract geometric ratio such as extreme and mean ratio (*Elements* VI, Def. 3) merely specifies *quid nominis* in thought; only when a rigorous compass-and-straightedge construction in *geometor.elements* or *geometor.model* actually brings the dividing point into existence—and *geometor.divine* verifies exact algebraic identity in act—is mathematical *esse* demonstratively established from first principles such as *Every whole is greater than its part*.
