Usage
=====

.. rubric:: Navigating the Semantic Digital Archive

This archive is built with `PHOTON Publish <https://github.com/photon-platform/publish>`_ as an interconnected semantic book codex.

Navigation
----------

* **Header & Sidebar**: Browse top-level works, **People**, **Bibliography**, **Concordance**, **Glossary**, **About**, and **Log** directly from the sidebar.
* **Prev / Next & Breadcrumbs**: Step sequentially through chapters, propositions, and sections using the top breadcrumb trail and bottom **Prev / Next** controls.
* **Track Switcher**: Where multiple witnesses or commentaries exist (such as **English**, **Greek**, **Latin**, **German**, **French**, **Italian**, **Summary**, and **Notes**), switch tracks at the top of the page.

Collections & Directories
-------------------------

* **People & Reference Directories**: Use the live search box, sort selector, and toggleable language/group pills on **People** and **Books / References** pages to filter records dynamically.
* **Layout Switcher**: Toggle standard collections between **Cards**, **Banners**, and **List** views.

Search & Tags
-------------

* **Full-Text Search**: Use the sidebar search box (or press ``/``) to search across titles, propositions, apparatus entries, and commentary.
* **Cross-References & Tags**: Click inline ``:person:``, ``:term:``, ``:cite:``, and geometric entity links to inspect definitions, biographical records, and cross-corpus citations.
