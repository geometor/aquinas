Commentary & Notes — Whether Mathematical Consideration Is Without Motion and Matter Concerning Things That Exist in Matter (On Intelligible Matter)
====================================================================================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Aristotelian Sources, Euclidean Counterparts, Hoenen's Noetics, and GEOMETOR Significance*

Aristotelian & Scholastic Foundations
-------------------------------------

This article—universally recognized as Aquinas's masterwork on the philosophy of mathematics—weaves together *On the Soul* III.6 (430a26–b6, the *understanding of indivisibles* versus composition and division), *Physics* II.2 (193b31–35, *abstrahentium non est mendacium* and the contrast between *curvum* and *simum*), *Metaphysics* VII.10–11 (1036a9–12, *hyle noete* or *intelligible matter* as what is present in sensible things not insofar as they are sensible, such as mathematical objects), *Categories* 6 (5a15–37, quantity having *situs* or relative position of parts), and *Posterior Analytics* II.11 (94a28–34, the angle in a semicircle proved right from two half-right angles as a material-cause demonstration). Remarkably, Aquinas's autograph manuscripts in Vatican Latin MS 9850 reveal multiple painstaking drafts of this exact *responsio*, wherein he perfected the terminological triad of *separatio*, *abstractio formae*, and *abstractio totius* to supersede earlier Boethian and Avicennian accounts.

Euclidean & Classical Geometric Counterparts
--------------------------------------------

Every major structural feature of :person:`Euclid <euclid>`\ 's *Elements* finds its philosophical warrant in Question V, Article 3. In *arg. 3* and *ad 3*, Aquinas addresses how the geometer numbers distinct straight lines of the same species to construct an equilateral triangle (*Elements* I.1) or a square (*Elements* I.46), grounding congruent multiplicity in *situs*—the order of parts intrinsic to dimensive quantity (*Categories* 6, mirrored in *Elements* I, Common Notion 4 on superposition and congruence). In *co. 4*, Aquinas contrasts *parts of the species* (the three bounding straight lines essential to a triangle in *Elements* I, Def. 19) with *parts of matter* (the accidental division of a circle into semicircles in *Elements* I, Def. 18). And in *arg. 4* and *ad 4*, he explains :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`\ 's famous example corresponding to *Elements* III.31—that the angle in a semicircle is a right angle—by showing that decomposing a geometric whole into quantitative sub-angles argues not from physical matter, but from *intelligible matter*.

Hoenen's De Noetica Geometriae & Judgment Theory
------------------------------------------------

In Chapter IV of *De Noetica Geometriae* (1954, p. 131, fn. 2; cf. Chapter II on *ST* I, q. 85, a. 1, ad 2), :person:`Hoenen <hoenen>` transcribes *Super Boetium De Trinitate* q. 5, a. 3, ad 3 verbatim as a cornerstone of geometric epistemology. Against Kant's claim that arithmetic and geometry require an *a priori* subjective form of pure sensibility to account for congruent multiplicity (such as distinct congruent triangles or enantiomorphs), Hoenen demonstrates from Aquinas's text that *materia intelligibilis*—dimensive quantity abstracted from sensible qualities by *abstractio formae* while retaining *situs* (the order of parts in homogeneous extension)—is objectively abstracted directly from sensible :term:`phantasms <phantasma>`. Furthermore, Hoenen links Aquinas's twofold operation of the intellect (*co. 1*) to his own central thesis in *La théorie du jugement*: simple apprehension abstracts the nexus of quantitative features in *intelligible matter*, while judgment affirms its necessary formal necessity.

GEOMETOR Constructive & Symbolic Significance
---------------------------------------------

Question V, Article 3 provides the exact ontological charter for GEOMETOR's symbolic engine. In *geometor.model*, points, lines, and circles are stripped of all *sensible matter*—floating-point measurement noise, physical thickness, and material perturbation—through a computational realization of *abstraction of form*, while preserving *intelligible matter* and *situs*: distinct lines and circles of identical species are individuated and numbered precisely by their exact algebraic order and positional incidence within the plane, allowing multiple equilateral triangles and squares to be constructed and compared. Moreover, Aquinas's defense of geometric part-whole demonstrations in *intelligible matter* (*ad 4*, illustrated by the semicircle theorem of *Elements* III.31) mirrors how *geometor.divine* and *geometor.elements* analyze complex polygons and harmonic ranges through exact algebraic part-whole decompositions in SymPy.
