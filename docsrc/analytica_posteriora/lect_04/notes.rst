Commentary & Notes — What Unqualified Scientific Knowledge Is and How Demonstration Proceeds from Primary, True, Immediate Causes
=================================================================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Aristotelian Sources, Euclidean Counterparts, Hoenen's Noetics, and GEOMETOR Significance*

Aristotelian & Scholastic Foundations
-------------------------------------

Expositing *Posterior Analytics* I.2 (71b9–29), Aquinas displays classic Scholastic mastery by uniting *Physics* II.9 and *De Anima* I.1 (the final-cause definition as middle term proving the material-cause definition) with *Metaphysics* II.1 (993b30, the convertibility of *esse* and truth). Following :person:`Themistius <themistius>` and Grosseteste on 71b17 (*syllogismus scientialis* rendering *syllogismos epistemonikos*), Aquinas carefully distinguishes knowing *simpliciter* from sophistical knowing *per accidens* (the Coriscus fallacy of *Sophistical Refutations* 24). Most importantly, in paragraph 16, Aquinas resolves the apparent contradiction between *Posterior Analytics* I.2 (71b33–72a5, universals are remote from sense and prior by nature) and *Physics* I.1 (184a16–26, universals are better known to us), establishing a decisive epistemological boundary between natural philosophy and mathematics.

Euclidean & Classical Geometric Counterparts
--------------------------------------------

Aquinas explicitly invokes *Book X of Euclid* (*ut patet ex X Euclidis*, referencing the classic proof associated with *Elements* X.2, X.9, and the ancient appendix X.117) to illustrate :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`\ 's example at 71b26 of an impossible falsehood that can never be scientifically known: that the diagonal (*diametrum*) of a square is commensurable (*symmetrum*) with its side. Defining incommensurable magnitudes with precision as quantities admitting no common measure and lacking the ratio of a number to a number (√2 : 1), Aquinas shows how geometric truth is anchored in objective quantitative being. Furthermore, :person:`Euclid <euclid>`\ 's axiomatic order exemplifies how in mathematics, because :term:`sensible matter <materia-sensibilis>` is abstracted away, formal definitions and axioms are simultaneously most intelligible *to us* and primary *in nature*.

Hoenen's De Noetica Geometriae & Judgment Theory
------------------------------------------------

:person:`Hoenen <hoenen>` repeatedly returns to Aquinas's teaching here (alongside *In I Anal. Post.*, lect. 10 and *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 85, a. 1, ad 2 in *De Noetica Geometriae* Chapters II and VI) to explain the unique transparency of geometric causality. Unlike physical substances whose inner essences remain hidden behind sensible accidents and must be deciphered *a posteriori* from effects, mathematical forms (*numeri, dimensiones, figurae*) are abstracted from sensible qualities while retaining :term:`intelligible matter <materia-intelligibilis>`; hence the formal nexus whereby properties flow *per se* from quantitative essence—including the exact crisis of incommensurability analyzed by Hoenen in Chapter VI—is directly penetrable to human intellectual intuition.

GEOMETOR Constructive & Symbolic Significance
---------------------------------------------

Aquinas's invocation of the incommensurability of the square's diagonal with its side (*Elements* X) strikes at the very heart of GEOMETOR's founding philosophy. Standard dynamic geometry software represents √2 and the golden ratio φ = (1 + √5)/2 as truncated binary rationals—effectively treating incommensurable magnitudes as *commensurable* ratios of integers and thereby embedding the very falsehood Aquinas declares unknowable. By grounding *geometor.model* in SymPy's exact symbolic algebra, GEOMETOR preserves the algebraic identity of quadratic and higher polynomial irrationals, ensuring that computational verification aligns with *being* and *truth*.
