  • GEOMETOR Architectural Significance: Explains pre-propositional structural recognition of geometric symmetries in geometor.explorer and geometor.divine.

aquinas:st:I.q84.a6 — I.q84.a6¶

  • Aristotelian Foundation: De Anima III.4–5, 429a10–430a25

  • Euclidean Counterpart: Origin of geometric concepts neither from Platonic innate ideas nor from Democritean sense impressions alone, but via intellectus agens illuminating phantasms

  • Codex Edition: Unabridged in English (pars-5) · Diplomatic Latin

  • Cited in Hoenen (1954): Caput III p. 82 (S. Th. I. q. 84 a. 6 in fine)

  • GEOMETOR Architectural Significance: Rejects both blind pixel empiricism and ungrounded formalism in favor of constructive insight (geometor.seer + geometor.model).

aquinas:st:I.q84.a6.ad3 — I.q84.a6.ad3¶

  • Aristotelian Foundation: De Anima III.7, 431a16–17

  • GEOMETOR Architectural Significance: Directly describes the human user forming diagrams in geometor.explorer to behold universal truths.
