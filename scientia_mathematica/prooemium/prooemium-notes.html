Prooemium — Editorial & GEOMETOR Notes¶

From Medieval Scholasticism to Symbolic Constructive Geometry

1. The Historical Arc: Aristotle -> Euclid -> Aquinas -> Pacioli -> Hoenen -> GEOMETOR¶

Within the geometor workspace, geometor-aquinas bridges the classical mathematical canon (euclid, elements, pappus, pacioli) and the modern epistemological synthesis of Peter Hoenen (hoenen). When Fra Luca Pacioli argued in De Divina Proportione (1509) that the mathematical sciences occupy the primo grado di certezza (‘first degree of certainty’), he stood directly inside the tradition of Aquinas‘s Super Boetium De Trinitate q. 6 a. 1 and In I Anal. Post. lect. 1. Four and a half centuries later, confronting Hilbert‘s formalist axiomatics and non-Euclidean geometry, Hoenen returned to these exact Thomistic texts—citing 78 distinct Thomistic loci—to prove that formal rigor and spatial intuition are allies rather than rivals.

2. Computational Mapping to the GEOMETOR Engine¶

  • Exact Symbolic Algebra as Materia Intelligibilis: Unlike floating-point dynamic geometry tools whose coordinates drift by numeric epsilon (like lines drawn in sensible dust), geometor.model represents every point, line, and circle over exact algebraic number fields in SymPy—preserving $\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{3}$, and the golden ratio $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$ in pure intelligible matter.

  • The Interactive Canvas as Phantasma: In exact fidelity to conversio ad phantasmata (De Anima III, lect. 12) and terminari ad imaginationem (De Trinitate q. 6 a. 2), geometor.explorer projects those exact symbolic structures onto an interactive SVG canvas where human geometric intuition inspects determinate diagrams.

  • Constructive Discovery by Division in Act: Just as Metaphysics IX, lect. 10 (Cathala #1894) celebrates geometers discovering latent truths dividendo in actu, geometor.divine systematically scans newly constructed intersections across the DAG to bring latent golden sections and harmonic ranges into luminous act.
