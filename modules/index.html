GEOMETOR • aquinas — Opera de Scientia Mathematica et Materia Intelligibili.

Critical bilingual Book Codex, philological apparatus, and Hoenen–Aquinas–Aristotle–Euclid concordance for St. Thomas Aquinas’s foundational writings on mathematical science, intelligible matter (materia intelligibilis), abstraction, heuristic division in act, and mathematical judgment terminating at the imagination.
